Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Summary

indchat is not launched yet. Right now we collect one thing: the email address you give us to join the early-access list. We use it to confirm your request and to tell you when your invite is ready.

What we collect

  • Email address, which you enter in the early-access form.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, which our hosting provider logs for security and reliability.
  • Messages you send us through the contact page or by email.

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To send your confirmation email and early-access invite.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data and we do not use your email for anything unrelated to indchat.

Who handles your data

We use service providers to run the site, store the early-access list and send email. They process data only on our instructions. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you ask us to remove it, or until twelve months after launch, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails. Write to privacy@indchat.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

Children

indchat is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell early-access members by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@indchat.com.
